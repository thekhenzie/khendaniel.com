About me.

I’m Khen Daniel (Harold Khen Daniel Baniqued on paper, but Khen is fine), a software engineer from the Philippines with about ten years of experience.

I’m currently at GuardHouse, where I build workforce management software for security companies in Australia and the UK.

Outside work I like building small, single-page tools for myself and the people around me: a 3D bed planner when we were rearranging furniture, a seating planner for our wedding, a pick board for League of Legends Worlds. They’re all on the projects page.

I also take photos, mostly of Manila’s skyline, sunsets and streets. Some of them are on this site, and more are in my photography portfolio.
