My Worlds 2026 predictions

12 October 2026

It's Worlds time again! The League of Legends World Championship 2026 runs from October 15 to November 14 in the US, with the final at Barclays Center in New York. Like every year, I'm doing the Pick'Ems and the Crystal Ball. This time I went a bit overboard with the research, so I might as well share it.

All my picks are also on my Worlds 2026 Pick Board, where I'll be marking hits and misses as the tournament goes.

The setup

  • Patch 26.20 is the Worlds patch. It nerfs Cassiopeia, K'Sante, Ashe, Ambessa and Yunara, and buffs Lucian, Swain, Neeko, Kindred and a few others.
  • Fearless Draft is used in every Bo3 and Bo5. Once a team picks a champion in a series, they can't pick it again in that series. Expect a lot of different champions.
  • Format: a 4-team Play-In (all Bo5, double elimination), then a 16-team Swiss stage, then 8-team Bo5 knockouts.
  • Favorites: Gen.G (LCK champion) is the betting favorite at around 33%, followed by HLE (MSI 2026 champion), BLG and T1, the three-time defending champion.

My stats come mostly from gol.gg, plus Worlds history from 2022 to 2025.

Play-In bracket

The Play-In has four teams: KC (LEC), Cloud9 (LCS), FURIA (CBLOL) and MVK (LCP). Only one goes on to Swiss.

  • Upper semis: FURIA over MVK, KC over Cloud9.
  • Upper final: KC over FURIA.
  • Lower semi: Cloud9 over MVK.
  • Lower final: FURIA over Cloud9. This is my riskiest pick. FURIA beat Cloud9 5-0 in games at the Americas Cup this year, but Cloud9 is playing at home.
  • Final: KC goes to Swiss. They reached the EWC 2026 final and beat T1 on the way.

What's next

The Swiss draw is on October 18, after the Play-In. I'll do my Swiss and knockout picks then. Follow along on the pick board to see how many I get right. Or, more likely, how many I get wrong. 😅
